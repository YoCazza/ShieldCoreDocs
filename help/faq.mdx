<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is ShieldCore free?">
    Yes. The Free plan covers every module with limits. [Premium](/plans) raises the limits and adds extras like automatic backups and a custom bot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Premium cover all my servers?">
    No. A Premium license covers **one server**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can use the dashboard?">
    People who can manage the server on Discord. Some sensitive lists, such as trusted users in AutoMod and Anti-Nuke, can only be changed by the server owner or an administrator.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does ShieldCore say it cannot manage a role or member?">
    Discord only lets a bot manage roles below its own. Move the **ShieldCore** role higher in **Server Settings → Roles**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AutoMod or Anti-Nuke punished the wrong person. What now?">
    Add them to the bypass or exceptions list, and adjust the thresholds. A more relaxed preset in Anti-Nuke also reduces false alarms. See [AutoMod](/security/automod) and [Anti-Nuke](/security/anti-nuke).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my setup if Premium ends?">
    Nothing is deleted. Items above the free limit are kept but switched off. See [Free vs Premium](/plans).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I remove ShieldCore from my server?">
    Your data is kept for 31 days in case you come back. See [Data and privacy](/help/data-and-privacy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the bot's name and avatar?">
    On Premium, yes: use [bot identity](/settings/bot-identity), or a [custom bot](/custom-bot/overview) for a fully separate bot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which languages are supported?">
    The dashboard supports English, Dutch, German and French.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
