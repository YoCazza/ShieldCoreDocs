---
title: "Setup wizard"
description: "The four steps that get a new server protected."
---

When you open a server for the first time, ShieldCore walks you through four short steps. You can come back to it later from **Setup** in the sidebar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Language">
    Choose the dashboard language. You can change it later in [Settings](/settings/general).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bot check">
    ShieldCore checks that it has the permissions it needs and that its role is high enough in your role list.

    If the check fails:
    1. Open **Server Settings → Roles** in Discord.
    2. Find the **ShieldCore** role.
    3. Drag it above the roles ShieldCore must manage.
    4. Save, then press **Check again**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Protection">
    Choose how strongly ShieldCore should protect the server. This turns on [AutoMod](/security/automod) (spam, caps, invites, mass mentions, bad language) and [Anti-Nuke](/security/anti-nuke) (stops people who mass-delete or mass-ban) with matching settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Logs and roles">
    Pick the channel where ShieldCore reports what happens, and optionally your moderator roles. AutoMod skips moderators and they can see the log channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You are not locked in. Every choice here can be changed later on the AutoMod and Anti-Nuke pages.
</Tip>
