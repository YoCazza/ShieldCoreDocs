Start giveaways from the dashboard or with `/giveaway`. Each giveaway gets a **public live tracking page** where people can follow entries and status.

## Create a giveaway

1. Open **Giveaway** from the Modules page.
2. Choose **New giveaway** and configure it: prize, duration, channel and number of winners.
3. Pick the style: an **embed** (a cleaner card) or a **normal message**.
4. Send it directly to a channel.

## Manage giveaways

The sidebar lists your giveaways. Select one to see its **status, entries and management actions**. You can also manage them in Discord with `/giveaway`, including picking new winners.

<Tip>
  The public page shows the giveaway rules so entrants know what applies.
</Tip>
