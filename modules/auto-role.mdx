---
title: "Automatic Role"
description: "Give or remove roles automatically when someone joins."
---

Automatic Role applies roles to new members the moment they join, so you do not have to do it by hand.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the module">
    Turn on **Automatic Role** under **Modules**, then open its settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a rule">
    Choose **Add role** to give a role on join, or **Remove role** to take one away. You can create several rules, so one join can add more than one role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  ShieldCore can only assign roles that are **below** its own role in the role list. If a rule does not work, move the ShieldCore role higher.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Combine this with a [workflow](/automation/workflows) if you want conditions, for example only act when the account is younger than a number of days.
</Tip>
