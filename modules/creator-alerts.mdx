---
title: "Creator Alerts"
description: "Announce new YouTube videos, Twitch streams, website posts and Steam game news in Discord."
---

Creator Alerts posts a message in Discord when something new appears on a platform you follow.

## Platforms

| Platform | What you enter | When it alerts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **YouTube** | A channel name or URL | A new video is uploaded. |
| **Twitch** | A username or channel URL | The streamer goes live. |
| **RSS / Atom** | A feed link, or just a website address (ShieldCore finds its feed) | A new post appears. Checked every 30 minutes. |
| **Steam** | A Steam store link or the game's app ID | The game posts news or patch notes. Checked every 30 minutes. |

When you add an RSS feed or Steam game, existing posts are saved but not announced, so your channel is not flooded on day one.

## Add an alert

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a platform and verify">
    Press **Add Creator Alert**, pick the platform and enter the creator, feed or game. Press **Verify** so ShieldCore can confirm it exists before you continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a channel">
    Select the Discord channel to **send alerts to**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Choose **Message**, **Embed** or **Message + Embed**. For an embed you can set the title, description, color, thumbnail, author, URL, footer and a timestamp. Click a variable to insert it at the cursor. The **live preview** shows the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press **Save alert**. You can switch an alert off and on later without deleting it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For RSS and Steam posts you can use `{posttitle}`, `{postlink}`, `{postdescription}` and `{thumbnail}`.

An alert marked **Needs attention** has a problem, for example a deleted channel. Open it to fix it.

## Limits

Free servers can have 2 alerts; Premium servers 25. See [Free vs Premium](/plans).
