---
title: "Creator Alerts"
description: "Announce new YouTube videos and Twitch streams in Discord."
---

Creator Alerts posts a message when a creator you follow uploads a video or goes live.

## Add an alert

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a platform">
    Pick **YouTube** or **Twitch** and verify the creator before configuring the alert.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a channel">
    Select where the announcement is posted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the message">
    Edit the message or embed so it fits your community. Use the preview to check it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Free servers can have 2 alerts; Premium servers 25. See [Free vs Premium](/plans).
