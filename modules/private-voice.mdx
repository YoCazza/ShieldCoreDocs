---
title: "Private Voice"
description: "Let members create and manage their own temporary voice channels."
---

Members join a **trigger channel** and instantly get their own voice channel with a control panel. When the last person leaves, the channel can delete itself.

## Configure it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose where channels come from">
    Pick the **category** for new channels (they inherit its permissions) and the **trigger channel** members join to create one. If none exist, create them in Discord first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the starting point">
    - **Name template:** use `{username}`, `{displayname}` and `{servername}`.
    - **User limit:** 0 means unlimited, up to 99.
    - **Privacy:** *Public* (anyone who can see the category can join) or *Private* (locked and hidden; only the owner, invited members, moderators and admins get in). Owners can change this later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide what happens to channels">
    - **Auto delete** when the last member leaves.
    - **Transfer on leave:** the next eligible member becomes the owner.
    - **Allow claim:** members in a channel whose owner left can take over with the **Claim** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose owner controls">
    Allow owners to **rename**, **set a limit** and **lock** their channel. Discord allows two renames per 10 minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Administrators and the moderator role can always manage every temporary channel. Free servers can have 5 active private channels at once; Premium 50.
