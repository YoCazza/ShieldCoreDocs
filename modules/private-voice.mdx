---
title: "Private Voice"
description: "Let members create and manage their own temporary voice channels."
---

Members join a **join-to-create channel** and instantly get their own voice channel, with a **control panel** in the channel's chat. When the last person leaves, the channel can delete itself.

Private Voice is available on every plan: 5 temporary channels can be active at the same time on Free, 50 on Premium.

## Configure it

The page shows at the top how many channels your plan allows and anything that still needs fixing.

### Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Join-to-create channel** | Members who join this voice channel are moved into their own new channel. |
| **Category** | New channels are created here and inherit its permissions. |
| **Channel name** | Template with `{username}`, `{displayname}` and `{servername}`. A preview shows the result. |
| **User limit** | 0 means unlimited, up to 99. |
| **Privacy** | **Public**: anyone who can see the category can join. **Private**: locked and hidden; only the owner, invited members, moderators and admins get in. Owners can change this later. |

If no voice channel or category exists yet, create them in Discord first.

### Permissions

- **Roles that can use Private Voice:** with no roles selected, every member can create channels. Administrators and the moderator role can always use it.
- **Moderator role (optional):** members with this role can manage every temporary channel and always have access, even to private ones.

### Channel behavior

- **Delete empty channels:** remove a channel as soon as the last member leaves.
- **Transfer ownership when the owner leaves:** the next eligible member in the channel becomes the owner.
- **Allow claiming abandoned channels:** members in a channel whose owner left can take over with the **Claim** button.
- **Creator becomes owner:** when off, channels start without an owner and can be claimed by anyone inside (if claiming is on).

### What owners may do

Choose which controls channel owners get:

- rename the channel,
- change the user limit (0 to 99),
- lock, unlock, hide and show,
- invite and remove members,
- transfer ownership,
- delete the channel.

Administrators and the moderator role can always manage every temporary channel.

## The control panel

Every temporary channel gets a panel in its chat. It shows the current state, for example *Locked · Hidden · Max 5*, and has buttons for:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Lock / Unlock** | Only invited members, the owner and staff can join while locked. |
| **Hide / Show** | Hide the channel from members who were not invited. |
| **Rename** | Change the name. Discord allows two renames per 10 minutes. |
| **Limit** | Set the maximum number of members (0 = unlimited). |
| **Invite** | Give someone access. They get a DM with a link to the channel. |
| **Remove** | Take someone's access away and disconnect them. |
| **Transfer** | Make another member in the channel the owner. |
| **Claim** | Take over a channel whose owner left. |
| **Delete** | Delete the channel. |
| **Staff** | Extra controls for administrators and the moderator role. |

## When a channel cannot be created

If a member joins the join-to-create channel but no channel can be made, ShieldCore tells them why in the voice channel's chat (or by DM): they do not have an allowed role, all channels on your plan are in use, or there is a setup problem. Staff see the exact cause on the dashboard.

ShieldCore needs **Manage Channels**, **Manage Roles**, **Move Members**, **View Channel** and **Connect** in the Private Voice category.
