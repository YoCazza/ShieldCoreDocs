---
title: "Tickets"
description: "Private support tickets opened from a panel, handled by staff and saved as transcripts."
---

Members open a private ticket from a **panel** (a message with a button or menu). Staff handle it in the ticket channel, and when it is closed the conversation is saved as a **transcript**.

Open **Tickets** in the sidebar. It has three parts: **Configuration**, **History** and **Analytics** (analytics is not available yet).

## Panels

A panel is the message members click to open a ticket. Create a panel, choose its categories or options and post it in a channel. Free servers can have 1 panel; Premium servers 25. The page shows how many you have used.

<Note>
  Deleting a panel removes it permanently, but tickets that are already open are not affected. The message already posted in Discord is not removed automatically and its buttons stop working.
</Note>

## Configuration (applies to all panels)

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Creator can close** | Allow the person who opened a ticket to close it. When off, only authorized staff can. |
| **Delete delay** | How long a closed ticket channel stays visible before it is deleted. |
| **Auto-close (Premium)** | Close tickets that have been idle for 1–24 hours. |
| **Transcript logging** | Choose whether transcripts are created. |
| **Transcript destination** | Post the transcript in the ticket before closing, and/or send it to a transcript channel. |
| **DM the creator** | Also send the ticket creator a private copy of the transcript. |

## Working a ticket

Staff can **claim** a ticket so everyone knows who is handling it, and use `/ticket` in the ticket channel to manage it.

## History and transcripts

**History** lists tickets with their status, category, who claimed them and whether a transcript exists. Search by creator or category, open a ticket to see its details, and view the transcript as a Discord-style conversation you can search. You can download all transcripts matching your filters as one ZIP file (the newest ones if there are very many).
