---
title: "Auto Responder"
description: "Reply automatically when a message contains a trigger word."
---

Auto Responder watches messages for words you choose and answers with a **reaction**, a **message** or an **embed**.

## Create a rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a rule">
    Open the module settings and create your first rule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the trigger">
    Enter the word or phrase that should start the response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the response type">
    - **Reaction:** react with an emoji.
    - **Message:** send text back.
    - **Embed:** send an embed card. Use the live preview to check it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a cooldown">
    The cooldown limits a rule to one response per channel in that time. Use 0 to disable the limit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **rule overview** lists all rules so you can edit or remove them.

<Tip>
  Great for FAQs: reply to "how do I join" with a short embed that links to your rules channel.
</Tip>
