Levels rewards active members. Everyone earns XP by chatting, levels up over time and can show off a rank card with `/levels`. You decide where XP counts, which roles members get at certain levels and how the rank card looks.

Levels is **free** for every server.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Open **Modules**, switch on **Levels** and open its settings. The module page only shows the settings once the module is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the level-up message">
    Decide what happens when someone reaches a new level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add role rewards (optional)">
    Give roles automatically at certain levels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design the rank card (optional)">
    Make the `/levels` card match your server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Changes reach the bot within a minute.

## How XP works

- Every message gives **15 to 25 XP**, at most **once per minute** per member. Spamming does not help; chatting regularly does.
- Bots, webhooks and system messages never earn XP.
- The next level costs **5 × level² + 50 × level + 100** XP. The first levels go fast and higher levels take longer, so active members always have something to work towards.

| Level | XP for this level | Total XP | About this many active minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 100 | 100 | 5 |
| 2 | 155 | 255 | 13 |
| 5 | 380 | 1,150 | 58 |
| 10 | 955 | 4,675 | 234 |
| 20 | 2,855 | 23,850 | 1,192 |
| 30 | 5,755 | 67,525 | 3,376 |
| 50 | 14,555 | 268,375 | 13,419 |

"Active minutes" assumes one message per minute at an average of 20 XP.

## Level-up message

Choose where ShieldCore announces a new level:

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Same channel** | The message appears in the channel where the member just chatted. |
| **Fixed channel** | Every announcement goes to one channel you choose. |
| **Direct message** | The member gets a DM. |
| **Off** | No announcement. |

You can write your own text with these variables: `{user}` (mention), `{username}`, `{level}` and `{server}`.

## Role rewards

Give a role automatically when a member reaches a level, for example *Regular* at level 5 and *Veteran* at level 20. You can add up to **25** rewards.

**Keep earlier reward roles** decides what happens with older rewards:

- **On:** members collect every reward role they earned.
- **Off:** members only keep the role of their highest reward.

<Warning>
  ShieldCore's own role must be **above** every reward role, or it cannot hand them out. See the [setup wizard](/dashboard/setup-wizard).
</Warning>

## Where XP counts

- **No XP in these channels:** for example spam, bot or counting channels. Choosing a category covers every channel in it, and a channel also covers its threads. Up to 50.
- **No XP for these roles:** for example a muted role. Up to 50.

## XP multipliers

Members with certain roles earn more XP per message. Add up to **10** multipliers between **×1.1** and **×5**. There is also a ready-made option for **Server Boosters**. Multipliers do not stack: only the highest one a member has counts.

## Wipe XP when a member leaves

Keeps the leaderboard clean. Someone who leaves and comes back starts again at level 0.

## Rank card

The rank card is the picture members get with `/levels`. A live preview shows your own Discord name and avatar.

- **Layout:** Classic, Mirrored or Compact.
- **Move parts:** drag the avatar, name, level, rank, XP and bar to where you want them. They snap to the middle and to each other; hold **Alt** to place them freely.
- **Per part:** show or hide it, and change size and alignment. The bar has a width and height, and the level can be a badge or plain text.
- **Avatar:** circle, rounded or square shape, with an optional accent ring and a glow behind it.
- **Colors and font:** background (optionally a gradient), accent, text, soft text and the empty part of the bar.
- **Background image:** upload a PNG, JPEG or WebP. It is cropped to 934 × 282 and saved right away. You can darken it so text stays readable.

**Default look** puts everything back to the standard ShieldCore card.

## Leaderboard

The module page shows a leaderboard of every member with XP, with pages. In Discord, members use `/leaderboard` to see the top 10 per page.

## Reset all XP

Use this for a new season, for example. It deletes the XP and levels of **every member of this server** and empties the leaderboard. Reward roles members already have are **not** removed.

<Warning>
  A reset cannot be undone. You have to type `RESET` to confirm. Only this server is affected.
</Warning>

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/levels [user]` | Show your own rank card, or that of another member. |
| `/leaderboard [page]` | Show the members with the most XP, 10 per page. |

If the module is off, these commands tell members that an admin can switch it on in the dashboard.

## Custom bots

Levels also works when your server runs on a [custom bot](/custom-bot/overview).
