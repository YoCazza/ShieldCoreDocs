---
title: "Reaction Roles"
description: "Let members choose their own roles from a message."
---

Post a message where members can toggle roles themselves. You choose how they pick:

| Style | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Buttons** | Each role is a button on the message. |
| **Reactions** | Members react with an emoji to get the role. |
| **Dropdown** | Members choose from a dropdown menu. |

## Create one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the message">
    Choose the channel and whether the message is a **normal message** or an **embed**. Write the content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add role items">
    Add each role with its label or emoji, under **Role items**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Save to post the message in Discord.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The ShieldCore role must be above every role you offer, or members will not receive them.
</Warning>
