---
title: "Suggestions"
description: "Collect member ideas with votes and a discussion thread."
---

Members share ideas with `/suggest`. Each idea appears as an **embed with vote buttons** and gets its own **discussion thread**, so feedback stays organized.

## Set it up

1. Enable **Suggestions** under **Modules**.
2. Open its settings and choose the channel where suggestions are posted.
3. Save, then tell members to use `/suggest`.

<Tip>
  Pin a short note in the suggestions channel that explains how to vote and how you handle ideas.
</Tip>
