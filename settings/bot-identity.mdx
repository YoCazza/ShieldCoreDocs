title: "Bot identity"
description: "Change how ShieldCore looks in your server: nickname, avatar, banner and bio."
---

Under **Settings → Bot** you can change how ShieldCore appears in your server. This is available on **every plan**, including Free.

## What you can change

| Field | Limits |
| --- | --- |
| **Nickname** | Up to 32 characters. |
| **Avatar** | PNG, JPEG, WEBP, GIF or AVIF. Max 1.2 MB, 320 × 320 recommended. |
| **Banner** | PNG, JPEG, WEBP, GIF or AVIF. Max 2.4 MB, 960 × 360 recommended. |
| **Bio** | Your own text, up to 141 characters, followed by a fixed ShieldCore line. |

Upload an image or paste a URL. An editor lets you drag and zoom to crop it, and a **live preview** shows your bot's profile as Discord will display it.

### The ShieldCore line in the bio

The bio always ends with:

```
Protected by ShieldCore · https://shieldcore.app
```

This line is what makes bot identity free. It cannot be removed or changed. Discord allows 190 characters in a bio, so 141 are left for your own text. The preview shows the bio including this line.

## Syncing to Discord

After you save, the change is **queued for sync**. Discord rate limits can make it take a moment. The sync status shows whether it is saving, queued or applied.

## With a custom bot

When your server runs on a [custom bot](/custom-bot/overview), bot identity does not apply. The Bot tab then tells you to change the name, picture, banner and bio of your own bot in the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications). Your own bot has no ShieldCore line in its bio.

<Tip>
  Want your own bot name in the member list, without the ShieldCore line? Use a [custom bot](/custom-bot/overview) (Premium).
</Tip>
