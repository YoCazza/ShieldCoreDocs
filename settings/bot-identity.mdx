---

<Note>
  Bot identity is a **Premium** feature.
</Note>

Under **Settings → Bot** you can customize how ShieldCore appears on Discord in your server.

## What you can change

| Field | Limits |
| --- | --- |
| **Nickname** | Up to 32 characters. |
| **Bio** | Up to 190 characters. |
| **Avatar** | PNG, JPEG, WEBP, GIF or AVIF. Max 1.2 MB, 320×320 recommended. |
| **Banner** | PNG, JPEG, WEBP, GIF or AVIF. Max 2.4 MB, 960×360 recommended. |

Upload an image or paste a URL. An editor lets you drag and zoom to crop the image, and a **live preview** shows your bot's profile as Discord will display it.

## Syncing to Discord

After you save, the change is **queued for sync**. Discord rate limits can make it take a moment. The sync status shows whether it is saving, queued or applied.

<Tip>
  Want a completely different bot name in the member list and the ability to invite your own application? Use a [custom bot](/custom-bot/overview).
</Tip>
