title: "Subscription"

---

**Settings → Subscription** shows your server's plan and, if it is Premium, the details.

## What you see

- **Plan:** Free or Premium, and the source (paid, partner, campaign or given by the team).
- **Time remaining**, **renewal date** or **access until**.
- **Billing:** monthly, yearly or lifetime.
- **Status:** active, trial or payment overdue.

## Manage your subscription

Press **Manage subscription** to open the secure billing portal, run by Creem. There you can update your payment method, view invoices, cancel or renew.

<Note>
  Only the person who purchased the license can see billing details and manage the subscription. Others see that Premium is active.
</Note>

## Partner, campaign and team Premium

These have no subscription to manage and end automatically on the date shown (lifetime partner Premium never ends). See [Free vs Premium](/plans) for what happens when Premium ends.
