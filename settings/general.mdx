Open **Settings** in the sidebar. The tabs are **General**, **Bot**, **Own bot**, **Subscription** and **Audit log**. This page covers **General**. Changes save automatically.

## Options

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Language** | The dashboard language: English, Dutch, German or French. |
| **Command prefix** | Used for message-based commands. Up to 16 characters, cannot be empty. |
| **Timezone** | Used by scheduled and time-based features such as [workflow schedules](/automation/workflows). |
| **Theme** | The look of your dashboard. Includes dark, light, midnight, slate, forest, amethyst, copper, OLED, nord, ocean, sunset, daylight, latte and high contrast. Some themes need Premium. |

<Note>
  Themes are a personal preference of your account, so they stay when a server's Premium ends.
</Note>
