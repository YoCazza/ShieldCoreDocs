## Before you ask

A good question gets a fast answer. Please include:

1. What you expected, and what happened instead.
2. The page or module involved, for example "AutoMod link filter".
3. Your **server ID** (shown at the top of the Settings page in the dashboard) if the bot is not responding.
4. Any error message or log code, plus a screenshot if it helps.

<Warning>
  Never share your bot token, passwords or login codes. ShieldCore staff will never ask for them.
</Warning>

## Quick checks

- **Bot not responding:** check that its role is high enough and that it can see the channel. Run `/ping` to see if it is online.
- **A module does nothing:** make sure it is switched on under **Modules** and that you saved your changes.
- **Plan limits:** you may have reached a [free plan limit](/plans).

## Billing

For payment questions, use **Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription** to reach the billing portal, or ask in the support server.
