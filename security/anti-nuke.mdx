title: "Anti-Nuke"
description: "Stop mass deletions, mass bans and permission abuse before they wreck your server."
---

A "nuke" is when someone with too much power (or a hijacked admin account or rogue bot) rapidly deletes channels and roles, mass-bans members or hands out dangerous permissions. Anti-Nuke watches for these patterns and reacts within seconds.

Open it from **Anti-Nuke** in the sidebar.

## Health check

At the top of the page, ShieldCore checks whether it can actually do its job. It warns you when:

- it is **missing permissions**, such as View Audit Log or Manage Roles. Without them it cannot stop people or restore things.
- **roles stand above the ShieldCore role** that still have dangerous permissions. Those members cannot be stopped. Move the ShieldCore role higher in **Server Settings → Roles**.

Press **Check again** after fixing it. When everything is fine you see one calm green line.

## Lockdown

During an attack, press **Start lockdown** (you are asked to confirm). ShieldCore then stops everyone from **writing, reacting, making threads and creating invites** at once, by changing what `@everyone` may do.

- **End lockdown** gives back exactly the permissions that were taken away.
- A lockdown ends by itself after **1 hour**.
- Channels with their own `@everyone` permissions are left alone.
- The bot picks up the request within a few seconds. If it has not after 20 seconds, the page tells you and lets you cancel the request, and shows the last error.

Lockdown also works for servers that run on a [custom bot](/custom-bot/overview).

## Presets

Choose a preset first, then fine-tune anything you like.

| Preset | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Relaxed** | Gives people more room before reacting. Fewer false alarms. |
| **Balanced** | A sensible middle. Reacts to clearly unusual activity. |
| **Strict** | Reacts after very few actions and uses harder punishments. For servers with a lot to lose. |

## Protections

Each protection triggers when a number of actions happen within a time window. These are the defaults:

| Protection | Triggers after | Default response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Channel create | 5 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions |
| Channel delete | 3 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions, restore |
| Role create | 5 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions |
| Role delete | 3 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions, restore |
| Webhook create | 5 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions |
| Emoji create | 6 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions |
| Emoji delete | 4 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions |
| Member ban | 5 in 10 s | Ban |
| Member kick | 5 in 10 s | Kick |
| Bot additions | 2 in 30 s | Kick |
| Server settings changes | 3 in 30 s | Remove dangerous permissions |
| Dangerous role permissions | 2 in 30 s | Remove dangerous permissions |

For every protection you can change the number of actions, the time window and the response, and switch it on or off. **Enable all** and **Disable all** change them in bulk.

A few extra things are watched too: **deleting webhooks** counts toward the webhook protection, a change of the **vanity URL** counts toward server settings changes, and giving `@everyone` the **Mention Everyone** or **Manage Channels** permission counts as a dangerous permission.

### Restore

For some protections ShieldCore can **undo the change automatically** when the protection triggers. Channel delete and role delete restore by default, so deleted channels and roles come back. You can switch on more (off by default):

| Protection | What is restored |
| --- | --- |
| Member ban | Lifts the bans handed out in the attack. |
| Server settings changes | Puts the server name, icon and verification level back. |
| Dangerous role permissions | Sets the role permissions back to what they were. |

## Alerts and lockdown

Optional, all off by default:

- **Send the server owner a DM** when someone is stopped. Nothing is sent if their DMs are closed.
- **Ping a role** in the Anti-Nuke log message, so your staff sees it.
- **Start a lockdown by itself** when several incidents follow each other quickly (2 to 10 incidents within 5 minutes), so the attack cannot go on under another account.

## Overview

The collapsible **Overview** at the bottom shows what Anti-Nuke stopped over the last 7, 30 or 90 days: incidents, actions caught, people stopped and things restored, per protection, per day, and the people stopped most often.

## Exceptions

Add people, roles and bots that Anti-Nuke must never punish under **Exceptions**. The server owner and ShieldCore are always trusted. Only the server owner or an administrator can change this list.

<Warning>
  Be careful who you trust. A trusted user or bot can delete channels without Anti-Nuke stepping in.
</Warning>

## Make sure it can act

ShieldCore can only punish members whose roles are **below** its own. Keep the ShieldCore role high in your role list. See the [setup wizard](/dashboard/setup-wizard).
