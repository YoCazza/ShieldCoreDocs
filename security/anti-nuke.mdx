---
title: "Anti-Nuke"
description: "Stop mass deletions, mass bans and permission abuse before they wreck your server."
---

A "nuke" is when someone with too much power (or a hijacked admin account or rogue bot) rapidly deletes channels and roles, mass-bans members or hands out dangerous permissions. Anti-Nuke watches for these patterns and reacts within seconds.

Open it from **Anti-Nuke** in the sidebar.

## Presets

Choose a preset first, then fine-tune anything you like.

| Preset | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Relaxed** | Gives people more room before reacting. Fewer false alarms. |
| **Balanced** | A sensible middle. Reacts to clearly unusual activity. |
| **Strict** | Reacts after very few actions and uses harder punishments. For servers with a lot to lose. |

## Protections

Each protection triggers when a number of actions happen within a time window. These are the defaults:

| Protection | Triggers after | Default response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Channel create | 5 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions |
| Channel delete | 3 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions, restore |
| Role create | 5 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions |
| Role delete | 3 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions, restore |
| Webhook create | 5 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions |
| Emoji create | 6 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions |
| Emoji delete | 4 in 10 s | Remove dangerous permissions |
| Member ban | 5 in 10 s | Ban |
| Member kick | 5 in 10 s | Kick |
| Bot additions | 2 in 30 s | Kick |
| Server settings changes | 3 in 30 s | Remove dangerous permissions |
| Dangerous role permissions | 2 in 30 s | Remove dangerous permissions |

For every protection you can change the number of actions, the time window and the response, and switch it on or off. **Enable all** and **Disable all** change them in bulk.

### Restore

For some protections (channel delete and role delete by default) ShieldCore can **undo the change automatically** when the protection triggers, so deleted channels and roles come back.

## Exceptions

Add people, roles and bots that Anti-Nuke must never punish under **Exceptions**. The server owner and ShieldCore are always trusted. Only the server owner or an administrator can change this list.

<Warning>
  Be careful who you trust. A trusted user or bot can delete channels without Anti-Nuke stepping in.
</Warning>

## Make sure it can act

ShieldCore can only punish members whose roles are **below** its own. Keep the ShieldCore role high in your role list. See the [setup wizard](/dashboard/setup-wizard).
