---
title: "Free vs Premium"
description: "What every server gets for free and what Premium adds."
---

ShieldCore is free to use. **Premium** is licensed **per server** and raises limits and unlocks extras. A Premium license covers one server.

## Comparison

| Feature | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ticket system | Yes | Yes |
| Ticket panels | 1 | 25 |
| Auto-close inactive tickets (1–24 hours) | No | Yes |
| Creator alerts (YouTube and Twitch) | 2 | 25 |
| Sticky messages | 2 | 25 |
| Sticky message footer | ShieldCore footer | Custom footer |
| Private voice channels (active at once) | 5 | 50 |
| Active workflows | 2 | 25 |
| Active custom commands | 2 | 25 |
| Saved backups | 1 | 25 |
| Manual backup cooldown | 24 hours | 30 seconds |
| Automatic backups | No | Every 4, 8, 12 or 24 hours |
| Automatic backup history | No | Latest 10 |
| Custom bot branding (nickname, avatar, banner, bio) | No | Yes |
| [Your own bot](/custom-bot/overview) | No | Yes |
| Dashboard themes | Fewer | More |

<Note>
  Limits can change. The live comparison on the [pricing page](https://shieldcore.app/pricing) is always the source of truth.
</Note>

## Billing

- Choose **monthly** (renews until you cancel) or **lifetime** (one payment, no renewal).
- Payments are handled securely by Creem. You manage your subscription from **Settings → Subscription**. See [Subscription](/settings/subscription).

## Other ways a server can have Premium

Premium can also come from a **partner** program, a **campaign** or be **given by the ShieldCore team**. These end automatically on the date shown in the dashboard, except lifetime partner Premium.

## When Premium ends

Nothing is deleted. ShieldCore keeps your setup and only applies the free limits again:

- The bot profile goes back to the default look. Your saved values are kept.
- Custom commands, workflows and sticky messages above the free limit are **kept but switched off**. The oldest stay on.
- You receive a message in your dashboard inbox.
