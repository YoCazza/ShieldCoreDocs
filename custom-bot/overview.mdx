<Note>
  The custom bot is a **Premium** feature. It is available with every Premium subscription, one bot per server.
</Note>

With a custom bot, ShieldCore runs under **your own Discord application**. Members see your bot's name and avatar instead of ShieldCore's, while all features keep working and you keep using the same dashboard.

## How it works

You create a bot in the Discord Developer Portal and give ShieldCore its **token**. ShieldCore then runs your bot in its own process for your server.

- The token is stored **encrypted** and is never shown again. You can replace or remove it any time.
- ShieldCore decides which bot is in charge of a server. Your own bot takes over when your server has Premium, your bot is online and it is in the server.
- Everything is managed under **Settings → Own bot**.

## What you get on the Own bot tab

| Area | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Status and buttons** | See whether your bot is running, and start or stop it. |
| **Set up** | A three-step guide: create the bot, invite it, start it. |
| **Move** | Step-by-step help to switch to your bot, or back to ShieldCore. |
| **Console** | A live view of what your bot is doing while it starts. |
| **Log** | A history of lifecycle events and errors. |
| **Settings** | Presence (status text with a preview), replace the token, remove the custom bot. |

## Good to know

- Your bot's **name and avatar** are set in the Discord Developer Portal.
- When moving messages, embeds, buttons and components are copied. File attachments are not.
- If your Premium ends, ShieldCore stops running your bot. See [Free vs Premium](/plans).
