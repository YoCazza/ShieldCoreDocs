---
title: "Set up your custom bot"
description: "Create your Discord application, add the token and start your bot."
---

You can do this from **Settings → Own bot → Set up**. The dashboard has an animated guide for the Developer Portal steps.

## Step 1: Create the bot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an application">
    Go to the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications) and choose **New Application**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    The name becomes your bot's name. You can change the avatar here too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Bot tab">
    Open **Bot** in the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on the intents">
    Turn on **Server Members Intent** and **Message Content Intent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the token">
    Select **Reset Token** and copy it. Treat it like a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in the dashboard">
    Paste the token on the Own bot tab. It is stored encrypted and not shown again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never share your bot token in Discord or screenshots. If it leaks, reset it in the Developer Portal and paste the new one in the dashboard.
</Warning>

## Step 2: Invite your bot

Use the **Invite** button, choose your server and accept. Your bot joins next to ShieldCore. Nothing changes for your members yet.

## Step 3: Start

Press **Start** and watch the **console**. Each step is shown live. If something goes wrong, you see what happened and how to fix it.

Once it is running, [move your messages](/custom-bot/moving) so buttons and panels belong to your bot.
