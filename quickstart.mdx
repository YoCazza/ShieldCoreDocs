## Before you begin

You need:

- A Discord account with the **Manage Server** permission (or ownership) on the server you want to protect.
- A few minutes to walk through the setup wizard.

## Set up ShieldCore

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the bot to your server">
    Open [shieldcore.app](https://shieldcore.app), log in with Discord and choose **Add to Discord**. Pick your server and accept the permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    After inviting the bot, select your server in the dashboard. The first time, ShieldCore starts the [setup wizard](/dashboard/setup-wizard) and keeps the rest of the sidebar locked until you finish it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix the role order if asked">
    ShieldCore can only manage roles below its own. In Discord, open **Server Settings → Roles** and drag the **ShieldCore** role above the roles it must manage, then press **Check again** in the wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a protection level">
    Pick how strongly ShieldCore should protect your server. This switches on [AutoMod](/security/automod) and [Anti-Nuke](/security/anti-nuke) with a matching preset. You can change every detail afterwards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a log channel and moderator roles">
    Choose where ShieldCore posts what happens, and optionally which roles are your moderators. Moderator roles are skipped by AutoMod and can see the log channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on your modules">
    Open **Modules** in the sidebar and enable what you need, for example [Tickets](/modules/tickets), [Welcome & Leave](/modules/welcome-leave) or [Giveaway](/modules/giveaway).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Create a [backup](/security/backups) once your server is set up the way you like it. If something ever goes wrong, you can restore from it.
</Tip>
