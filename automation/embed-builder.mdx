The Embed Builder helps you create polished embeds (the colored cards with a title, description, fields and images) and send them to a channel, with a live Discord-style preview.

Open **Embed Builder** in the sidebar (under **Builders**).

## What you can do

- Write the title, description, color, author, footer and images.
- Add **buttons** and other components to the message.
- Preview the result exactly as members will see it.
- Send it to any channel.

Embeds posted this way can also be moved to your own bot if you [switch to a custom bot](/custom-bot/moving).

<Tip>
  Use embeds for rules, announcements and info channels. Pair buttons with [Reaction Roles](/modules/reaction-roles) or [tickets](/modules/tickets).
</Tip>
