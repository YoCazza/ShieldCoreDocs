## How it works

1. **Add ShieldCore** to your server and log in to the [dashboard](https://shieldcore.app) with Discord.
2. **Run the setup wizard.** It checks permissions and turns on sensible protection.
3. **Enable the modules you need** and adjust them in the dashboard.
4. **Upgrade when you outgrow the limits.** Free is enough to start; [Premium](/plans) raises limits and unlocks extras.

<Note>
  Everything you configure in the dashboard applies to one server. If you manage several servers, switch between them from the server picker at the top left of the dashboard.
</Note>
